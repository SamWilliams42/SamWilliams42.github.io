You Enter a Cavern

This song was a project I made for someone else in FL Studio. It was made to be used as background music for a bossfight while playing a Tabletop Roleplaying Game. The boss was the oddly named Magnetic Gecko from Pathfinder 2nd Edition, and the fight was planned to take place in a cave.

When creating this song, my primary goal was to make something that could be listened to in the background for multiple hours, since TTRPG combats often take multiple hours to complete. For instrumentation, I wanted some metallic and echoey sounds, to evoke the feeling of a cave and to represent the Magnetic Gecko. Additionally, I tried to make the song feel slightly "off", to reflect the unusual nature of the fight.

I also created an extended version of the song that would cause fewer interruptions for extended listening, as well as a seamless looped version for if the listening device supports perfect loops. You can find them here: Seamless Loop | Extended Version
